Filling the Void Left by Rove Papers

For years, O-Level students counted on platforms like Rove Papers to access past examination papers easily. After its shutdown, students faced scattered PDFs and unstructured archives. O-Level Exam Paper Analyser was created to not only bring back past paper browsing, but to supercharge it with modern data analytics, topic frequency analysis, and automated syllabus intelligence.

1. Access All Papers

Search and filter Question Papers and Mark Schemes with 1-click preview and download capabilities directly formatted for desktop and mobile screens.

2. Understand Question Trends

Our extraction pipeline categorizes every past exam question against the exact official Cambridge syllabus unit and code, revealing mark distributions.

3. Plan Evidence-Based Revision

Target frequently tested areas first. Use backtested prediction insights and customized study roadmaps to prepare efficiently for May/June and Oct/Nov exam series.
